Frequently Asked Questions

Is this WebP image converter really 100% private?

Yes. OmniTool operates entirely within your local browser runtime. No images, files, or prompt strings are transmitted to remote servers, making it compliant with strict privacy standards.

Why should I convert my JPG and PNG images to WebP?

WebP is Google's modern image format that provides superior lossless and lossy compression. WebP images are 26% smaller than PNGs and up to 34% smaller than comparable JPEGs, dramatically speeding up website loading times and improving SEO scores.

How does the Instagram line break generator fix collapsed captions?

Instagram's algorithm automatically removes empty newlines. OmniTool replaces blank returns with clean zero-width invisible Unicode separator characters, preserving clean paragraphs without unsightly punctuation symbols.
